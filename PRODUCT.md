# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

android

## Users

People scanning or importing documents on an Android phone for personal use. The primary export path should favor quick results with sensible defaults; detailed settings remain available when needed.

## Product Purpose

Turn paper or imported images into reviewable files that can be saved and shared from the device.

## Capabilities and Constraints

ScanLite uses the Google document scanner or Android photo picker, supports JPEG, JPG, PNG and PDF exports, optional size limits, local history, and a user-selected save folder. It has no account, backend, analytics, advertisements, or cloud storage. Scanning depends on compatible Google Play services. Cropping and scanner filters belong to the external scanner flow.

## Product Principles

- Make the next action obvious and keep defaults useful.
- Let people review processed output before saving.
- Explain failures and offer practical recovery.
- Keep documents on the device and avoid broad storage permissions.
